## Observed results

There were 72 logical live Databricks calls: 36 training, 18 validation, and 18 test.
ReAnchor compilation made zero additional calls after the training and validation
responses were cached. This is specific to this single-predictor run; complex
programs can generate new requests when an upstream decision changes.

The baseline classified all training and validation emails correctly. ReAnchor
examined the training probabilities and retained threshold `0.5`; its report
states that the fitted behavior did not beat the original. All integration
checks passed, including preservation of probabilities, contextual policy
selection, and saving/restoring the resulting policy.

The business cost was 2 per missed reply and 1 per false alert:

| Policy | Test emails | Missed replies | False alerts | Total error cost |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Baseline: 0.5 | 18 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| Manual: 0.3 | 18 | 0 | 2 | 2 |
| ReAnchor: retained 0.5 | 18 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| Contextual: critical 0.3, standard 0.5 | 18 | 0 | 1 | 1 |

**This run demonstrates working integration, not an optimization gain.** The
lower manual threshold added a false alert without catching additional missed
replies. These synthetic results do not establish performance on a real inbox.
The dataset, labels, task, costs, and thresholds were not revised after seeing
the test results. The run records source and dataset hashes for inspection.

Development used DSPy 3.4.1 and Databricks SDK 0.157.0. Six focused offline
integration/arithmetic checks also passed. Files under `runs/` are local raw
run captures and are ignored by Git; `initial_run/` holds the selected evidence.
